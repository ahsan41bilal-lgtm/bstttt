Happy Birthday, Princess

For the girl who carries two little worlds and still makes everyone feel loved.

Open your letter

A letter for you

written with all my heart, just for you

Dear Princess,

Happy Birthday. Today the world celebrates the girl who gives and gives, and almost never asks for anything back.

You are a mama to two little hearts, and you carry their whole world on your shoulders. You do it alone, and you still smile for them.

I see it all. The early mornings, the late nights, the worry you hide, the strength you make look easy. I feel your hard work, even when you never say a word about it.

I really can't see you sad. It hurts me too. Your happiness matters to me more than I know how to say.

I pray your kids grow up healthy, kind and proud, and that they always know how lucky they are to have you. One day they will look back and understand everything you did for them.

Until then, let me remind you: you are doing beautifully. You deserve love, rest, laughter, and someone who makes your days lighter.

Happy Birthday, my princess. Stay happy, always.

Ahsan your prince
